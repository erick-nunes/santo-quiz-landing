Frequently asked questions

Is the app free? Yes. Santo Quiz is completely free with no ads and no in-app purchases.
What languages is the app available in? Portuguese, English and Spanish. You can switch anytime in Settings → Language.
Do I need the internet to play? Yes. The app fetches questions and saint images from the server so the content is always up to date.
What are the quiz modes? Three: Classic Quiz (questions about the Bible, saints, liturgy and doctrine at different difficulty levels), Mass Quiz (weekly quiz with questions tied to Sunday's liturgical readings) and Saints Quiz (fun card mode for recognizing saints by their images).
How does the Saints Quiz work? Tap the card to reveal the saint's name, feast day, patronage and a short biography. Swipe the card sideways to move to the next one. It's a game ideal for families or groups.
How does scoring work? Your score is the number of correct answers (e.g., 7 out of 10). Answering fast and without using hints earns more stars. Hints don't hurt your score — they're just there when you need them.
I found a question with wrong information. What should I do? Thanks for the feedback. Please email eu@ericknunes.dev with the quiz mode, the question and the suggested correction. We'll review and update.
Can I suggest new questions or saints? Of course. Suggestions are very welcome. Write to eu@ericknunes.dev with your suggestion. We check the sources and, if everything checks out, add it to the app in the next update.
Does the app collect my data? No. Santo Quiz does not collect any personal data, use analytics or trackers. See our Privacy Policy for details.
What devices does it work on? Android 7.0 or newer and iPhone/iPad with iOS 16.4 or newer.
The app crashed or won't open. What should I do? Try these steps in order: 1) close and reopen the app; 2) restart your device; 3) uninstall and reinstall the app from the store. If the problem persists, email eu@ericknunes.dev with your device model, OS version and a description of what happens.
