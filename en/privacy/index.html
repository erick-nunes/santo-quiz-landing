Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Santo Quiz. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information when you use our Catholic quiz app.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information we DO NOT collect

  • Personally identifiable data (name, email, phone).
  • Geographic location.
  • Contact information.
  • Personal photos or files.
  • Microphone or camera data.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Quiz scores and progress (stored locally on the device).
  • Game preferences (selected language and difficulty).
  • Basic technical data for the app to work (device type, operating system version).
  • Anonymous push notification token (with no personal identification), when the user opts in to receive notifications.

3. How we use information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Enable the app to function properly.
  • Save your progress and scores locally.
  • Improve user experience.
  • Deliver quiz questions.
  • Send push notifications about new quizzes (only if you opt in).

4. Data sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer your personal information to third parties.

The app only connects to our server to fetch quiz questions.

5. Data storage

  • All progress data is stored locally on your device.
  • We do not keep databases with users' personal information.
  • You can clear all app data through your device settings.

6. Security

  • Secure connections (HTTPS) with our server.
  • We do not collect sensitive data.

7. Children's data

Santo Quiz is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

  • Use the app without providing personal data.
  • Delete all local data by clearing the app cache.
  • Uninstall the app at any time.
  • Disable push notifications in system settings.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you about significant changes through an app update.

10. App permissions

  • Internet: to fetch quiz questions.
  • Network state: to check connectivity.
  • Notifications: to notify you about new quizzes (optional).
  • Audio: to play game feedback sounds.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback (optional).

11. Contact

  • Email: eu@ericknunes.dev
  • Website: santoquiz.com

12. Legal compliance

This policy complies with:

  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — European Union.
  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) — United States.
  • Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) — Brazil.

By using Santo Quiz, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
