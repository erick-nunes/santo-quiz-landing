Frequently asked questions

Is Santo Quiz free?

Yes, Santo Quiz is 100% free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. It is maintained by the author and by voluntary donations.

Which quiz modes are available?

Three modes: Classic Quiz (questions on the Bible, saints, liturgy and Catholic tradition), Sunday Mass Quiz (questions tied to this Sunday's readings, updated weekly) and Saints Quiz (recognize saints from their image, in a card-deck format).

Which devices does it work on?

Santo Quiz is currently available on iPhone and iPad via the App Store. The Android version on Google Play is coming soon.

Is the content reviewed by Catholics?

Yes, all content is written and reviewed by practicing Catholics, with sources in Scripture, the Catechism of the Catholic Church, the liturgy and Tradition.

Can I play as a family?

Yes, Santo Quiz is made to be played as a family — children, parents and grandparents. The Saints Quiz, with its card-deck image format, is especially good for playing in group.

How does the Sunday Mass Quiz work?

Every week a new quiz is published with questions based on the Sunday readings (first reading, psalm, second reading and Gospel). Perfect to prepare your heart before Mass or review it afterwards as a family.
